SYNCHRONY
Lag-corrected gesture and posture alignment between subjects. Measures movement rhythm, not just simultaneity. High synchrony = attunement, rapport, or mirroring dynamic.
Cross-correlation of posture signals over 2s window
Lag tolerance: ±400ms (accounts for social latency)
Output: 0.0–1.0 synchrony index per frame
Logs to .pat social_field.synchrony
MIRROR NEURONS
MIRRORING
Detects when one subject replicates gestures, expressions, or posture of another. Voluntary vs involuntary distinguished by latency. Involuntary = empathy. Voluntary = rapport building.
Latency <300ms = involuntary (empathy signal)
Latency 300–900ms = conscious mirroring
Source subject tracked per mirror event
Logs to .pat social_field.mirroring[]
RELATIONAL FIELD
ATTUNEMENT
Composite index combining synchrony, mirroring, gaze reciprocity, and co-regulation. Two attuned subjects show coordinated nervous system states — not just behavioral similarity.
Inputs: synchrony + mirroring + gaze reciprocity
Co-regulation: shared entropy slope trajectory
Output: attunement_index per 5s window
Feeds Sigma layer as social entropy modifier

Oppositional posture pairs logged as tension events
Concurrent avoidance + approach = high tension
Tension score: 0.0–1.0 per window

SIGMA LAYER · ENTROPY DETECTION · PROBABILISTIC BEHAVIORAL DEDUCTION
SLOPE DETECTION
SLOPE WINDOWRate of signal change computed over rolling 3s window. High slope = rapid behavioral shift = entropy flag.
THRESHOLDSlope ≥ 0.6 over window triggers dysregulation check. Configurable per session in .pat schema.
MULTI-LAYERSlope computed independently per signal layer. Simultaneous spikes across layers = stronger combined signal.
DEDUCTION ENGINE
MULTIPLE DEDUCTIONSEach observation produces N deductions. Never one answer. Always a probability distribution over states.
CONFIDENCE SCORE0.0–1.0 per deduction. Deterministic only at ≥ 0.95 and only for well-established signal combinations.
UNCERTAINTY AS DATAThe system holds uncertainty as signal — not noise to eliminate. Low-confidence deductions are still logged.
STATE VOCABULARY
DYSREGULATEDHigh entropy, multi-layer slope spike, low predictability. Multiple simultaneous dysregulation indicators.
UNGROUNDEDModerate entropy, postural instability, gaze dart, fragmented gesture sequence.
ACTIVATINGEntropy rising from stable baseline — mobilization, not dysregulation. Context-dependent.
STABLELow entropy, coherent posture, sustained gaze, regulatory behavior absent or minimal.
