VOICE CAPTURE
The orb (Voice Lattice) and the always-on listener below share these settings — everything transcribes through the same local model, in the same order it was spoken.
Rolling recall
Always listening in the background — captures the 3s BEFORE speech starts too, so the first word is never clipped waiting for you to press record. Off by default; this is a deliberate choice to make.
Sensitivity
How loud you need to be before it's treated as speech. Lower catches quieter speech but also more background noise.
0.018
Pause length
How long you need to go quiet before a thought is considered finished and sent to transcription.
1.2s
Pre-roll buffer
How far back each segment reaches before the moment speech was detected — this is the anti-clipping buffer.
0.8s
Transcription model
Larger models are more accurate and slower. Only a model you've already got downloaded will actually be used.
Language
Forcing your actual language skips whisper's own language-detection pass — slightly faster, and removes one place it can guess wrong on short segments. "Auto" detects per segment.
CPU threads
How many threads whisper.cpp uses per segment. "Auto" splits your CPU cores in half — enough to transcribe fast without starving the rest of the app. Raise it on a machine that's only running this.
auto
Transcription timeout
If a single segment hasn't finished transcribing by this point, whisper.cpp is killed and the segment is marked unreadable rather than left hanging the queue. Raise this for the Large model on a slow machine.
120s
Max segment length
A safety valve — if you talk this long without a pause, the segment is cut and transcribed anyway rather than growing without bound.
25s
Use your own vocabulary
Feeds Whisper your own distinctive words (foundation/ai/lexicon.js) as its prompt for every segment — the single largest accuracy gain for personal speech, proper nouns and jargon it would otherwise mishear. Same setting as "Prompt seeding" under Lexicon, shown here too since it's a Whisper option.
Vocabulary prompt size
How many of your most distinctive words to feed Whisper per segment. Whisper.js hard-caps this at 48 regardless of what's set here — an over-long prompt biases the model toward words you didn't say.
40
